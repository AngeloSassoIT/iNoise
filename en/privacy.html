Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Your privacy is at the heart of iNoise. In short: the app collects no personal data, doesn't track you and requires no account. iNoise runs entirely on your device and never talks to any server. This page explains what that means in detail.

Data we collect

None. iNoise does not collect, transmit or share personal data. We never ask for your name, email or any kind of sign-up to use the app.

Where your data lives

The only information the app stores is your usage preferences, and it stays entirely on your device:

  • the sounds you mark as favorites;
  • your chosen theme and light/dark mode;
  • your timer settings and default fade;
  • your last playback state.

This data is stored locally (using Apple's on-device storage) and never leaves your iPhone or iPad. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

No tracking

iNoise uses no tracking technology, third-party analytics or advertising identifiers. We don't profile your behavior and we don't share information with advertisers or data brokers. The app's bundled privacy manifest declares: no tracking and no data collected.

Device permissions

iNoise requests no system permissions: it doesn't access your photos, contacts, location, microphone, camera, calendar or notifications. The app only uses background audio playback, needed to keep playing when the screen is locked or you switch to another app.

System controls and playback

To show the title and controls (play/pause, previous/next) on the lock screen, in Control Center, on your earbuds and in CarPlay, iNoise integrates with the standard iOS audio features. This information stays on your device and is only used to control playback; it is never sent to us or to third parties.

Internet connection

The app needs no internet connection to work. Sounds are generated in real time on the device and no network calls are made to our systems or external services.

Children

iNoise collects no data from any user, including children. The app is suitable for all ages and can be used safely to help little ones fall asleep too.

Third-party services

iNoise integrates no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services. The only company involved is Apple, which distributes the app through the App Store under its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app's features ever change in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the “last updated” date above. We encourage you to review it from time to time.

Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, email us at support@simplebuild.it.
