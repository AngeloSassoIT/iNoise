Frequently asked questions

Is iNoise free? Are there ads or purchases?

Yes, iNoise is completely free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account required. Every feature is available from the start.

Are the sounds recordings?

No. Every sound is synthesized in real time by an audio engine running on your device. That's why the loop is truly endless: the audio is generated on the fly and never jumps or repeats, without the “click” typical of looped recordings.

Can I combine multiple sounds?

Yes. With the mixer you can play up to 3 sounds at once — for example rain, wind and warm white noise — adjusting each volume independently to craft your ideal ambience.

How does the sleep timer work?

You can set a duration (with quick presets or a stepper) or a stop time. When it ends, the audio fades out on its own with a smooth, adjustable fade, so you can drift off without a worry.

Does it keep playing on the lock screen or with other apps open?

Yes. iNoise plays in the background: you can lock the screen or use other apps. You'll find the controls (play/pause, previous/next) on the lock screen, in Control Center, on your earbuds and in CarPlay.

What happens during a call or when another app plays audio?

iNoise pauses automatically during an interruption (like a phone call) and resumes when possible. If you unplug your earbuds, playback stops so the sound doesn't suddenly blast from the speaker.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes, entirely. No connection is needed: the sounds are generated on your device and the app never talks to any server.

Does iNoise collect my data?

No. iNoise doesn't collect personal data, doesn't track you and doesn't request system permissions (photos, notifications, location, microphone). Your preferences — like favorites and theme — stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Do my favorites and settings sync across devices?

Not at the moment: favorites, theme and settings are stored locally on the device you use and aren't transferred automatically to other devices.

Which devices are supported?

iNoise is designed for iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 26 or later.

I have an idea for a sound. How do I suggest it?

We'd love that! Email us at support@simplebuild.it describing the sound you'd like: we consider requests for future updates.
